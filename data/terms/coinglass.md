# Coinglass terms check

Fetched: 2026-10-03 (ET), https://www.coinglass.com/terms

Decision: do not fetch, archive, or display Coinglass data on alpha.kurult.ai.

Operative text from that page, section 4, Data and Intellectual Property:

"You may not: Copy, sell, or redistribute data; Use the data for commercial purposes without authorization; Scrape, crawl, or extract data in bulk without permission."

Public display of their funding, open interest, or liquidation series would be redistribution. No written authorization is on file. Liquidations stay a gap until a source whose terms allow public display is saved here.
