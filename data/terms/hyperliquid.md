# Hyperliquid terms check

Fetched: 2026-10-03 (ET), https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/terms
Last updated on the page: June 15, 2026.

Decision: do not display Hyperliquid market data (funding, open interest, basis, liquidations, or HIP-3 status numbers) on alpha.kurult.ai.

The page is an interface Terms of Use. It describes a website-hosted interface. It does not grant a right to republish market data. No separate data license allowing public display was found in that fetch. Until one is saved in this directory, Hyperliquid figures stay off the site.

The 2026-10-02 note already says the HIP-3 launch was unconfirmed. That sentence is not a new data pull.
