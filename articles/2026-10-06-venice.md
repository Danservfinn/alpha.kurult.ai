## First principles: the inference margin

Venice's primitive is commodity inference with privacy plumbing. The product routes prompts to open-weight models, serves them without storing prompts, and meters API usage. The physical unit is a token of inference: GPU-seconds, electricity, bandwidth. The business buys compute wholesale and sells tokens retail. That is a margin business, and margin businesses live or die on the spread between wholesale compute cost and retail inference price.

The spread has been collapsing industry-wide. DeepSeek-class efficiency gains and falling GPU rental rates have cut wholesale inference cost dramatically. Venice's answer on the revenue side is the uncensored niche: near-zero refusal models that centralized labs will not serve. A willingness-to-pay exists there that commodity chat does not have. The $100M ARR run rate announced August 17 says the spread, for now, works at scale: 3.5M users, 1.3T monthly tokens, profitable since Q1 2026.

Follow the money to the token and the flywheel is mechanical. Subscriptions burn VVV directly: $2 per Pro, $5 per Pro+, $10 per Max, programmatic and auditable. Emissions are now 2M VVV a year, 100% to stakers, and 68% of float is staked for API capacity. So VVV demand is chained to product demand by construction: usage grows, subscriptions grow, burns grow, staking demand grows. No other AI token in this set has that link. The two facts that break the machine: over 97% of supply sits in 100 wallets, and a token audit flags an uncapped owner mint with no timelock. Either one firing turns the flywheel into an exit-liquidity event. That is why constructive has a defined-risk cap on it.

## Platform Overview
Venice AI, launched in May 2024 by ShapeShift founder Erik Voorhees, with president and CTO Jesse Proudman (the project grew out of the pair's earlier Morphic and Ninji AI ventures), is a privacy-first alternative to ChatGPT. It serves open-source and proprietary models for text, image, video, code, and agentic workflows through one web app and API, with no storage of user prompts or conversations on centralized servers. Its flagship in-house model, Venice Uncensored (a 24B Dolphin Mistral collaboration), plus derivatives like Gemma 4 Uncensored, offer near-zero refusal behavior, making Venice the leading "uncensored" consumer AI brand.

## Tokenomics
VVV launched on Base in January 2025 with a 100M supply. Since then 33.89M VVV (41.78% of original supply) has been burned, including all unclaimed airdrop tokens in March 2025. Emissions have been cut in a staircase from 14M per year to 2M as of October 1, 2026 (down 86%), with 100% of new tokens routed to stakers. Since December 2025, subscription revenue funds programmatic buy-and-burn: each new Pro, Pro+, and Max subscription triggers burns of $2, $5, and $10 respectively. Staking VVV (about 6% yield) grants a proportional daily share of API inference capacity; 33.21M of the 48.57M circulating supply is staked, and stakers can lock VVV to mint DIEM, each worth $1 of daily API credit in perpetuity (DIEM trades near $2,000).
