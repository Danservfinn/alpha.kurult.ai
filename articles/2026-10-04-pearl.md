## What Pearl is

Pearl is a proof-of-work Layer 1 derived from Bitcoin. Mainnet launched 2026-04-27. It replaces SHA-256 with noised matrix multiplication, so GPU work that serves AI inference can mine PRL at the same time. Pearl calls this "duplex compute". The core construction is in arXiv 2504.09971. Max supply is 2.1B PRL, with a smooth emission curve that puts half the supply out by block 650,226. The explorer reports a 194 second target and a current block reward of 2,282.69 PRL (pearlchain.live stats, 15:24 ET).

## First principles: what the scarce unit pays

The scarce unit is a winning noised matrix multiply, paid in new PRL. Fees are not the metric.

- At the 194 second target: about 445 blocks a day and 1,016,620 PRL a day, about $1,069,484 at $1.052. Explorer hashrate 48.18 EH/s (difficulty-derived, unconfirmed), so about $22,200 per EH/s per day. All-time fees 7,214 PRL, about $7,589 (0.002% of formula supply). [pearlchain.live stats 15:24 ET; CoinGecko 15:22 ET]
- Whitepaper (September 2026): each tile of the product "doubles as a lottery ticket for block production." That describes the FP8 spec. Mainnet is still INT-8. Weinstein (Sep 25): the live protocol "is INT-8 only, and adds 10-15% overhead," with Pearl-GEMM "expected to launch within weeks," and spam mining "will always" be allowed.
- Unit economics, one GPU-hour that could mine or be rented (ASSUMPTION scale from Basu's June 24 EH/s / 320,000 GPU / 112 MW census to 48.18 EH/s): new PRL about 7 cents per GPU-hour; May 31 vast.ai RTX 3090 median rental $0.34 (stale); protocol fee about $0.000003 per GPU-hour. Power cash cost per PRL about $0.13 to $0.76 (base $0.32 at $0.06/kWh). Rental cost per PRL about $1.94 to $6.19 across that band. Issuance is revenue to miners and dilution to holders. It is not protocol revenue.
- Reconciliation: Oct 1/2/3 explorer coins 1,065,348 / 897,103 / 994,445 (three-day average 985,632) versus target 1,016,620, gap about -3.1%, inside a 15% band.

## Catalysts and risks

Catalysts:

1. FP8 / Pearl-GEMM hardfork. Still "within weeks" as of Sep 25, with no date. Main fundamental catalyst. PR 311 open on Oct 4.
2. A second named duplex partner, or Together's endpoint becoming clearly live on serverless.
3. A venue with real depth (or a confirmed tier-1 listing). None in the Oct 4 ticker pull.
4. Attestable proof that a material share of hashrate does paid inference. Fees above 1% of block rewards for 30 days would be an early sign.

Risks:

- Dilution: +26% supply in 3 months and +92% in 12 (unconfirmed supply path).
- Thin, low-trust venues; issuance already half of reported volume.
- Pool concentration near 50%.
- Launch-week supply concentration.
- Rentable, merge-mined hashrate, and two consensus incidents already (July MoE disable; August salted noise-seed hardfork).
- Ticker collisions.
- Upgradeable-bridge claim carried, not re-verified.
