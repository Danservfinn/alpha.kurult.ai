## What Pearl is

Pearl is a proof-of-work Layer 1 derived from Bitcoin. Mainnet launched 2026-04-27. It replaces SHA-256 with noised matrix multiplication, so GPU work that serves AI inference can mine PRL at the same time. Pearl calls this "duplex compute". The core construction is in arXiv 2504.09971. Max supply is 2.1B PRL, with a smooth emission curve that puts half the supply out by block 650,226. The explorer reports a 194 second target and a current block reward of 2,282.42 PRL (pearlchain.live stats, 17:29 ET, height 123,245).

## Catalysts and risks

Catalysts:

1. FP8 / Pearl-GEMM hardfork. Still described as "within weeks" in the unverified Sep 25 post, with no date. PR 311 is open, not merged. Its pearl-gemm kernels, head cb102159, declare three families only: SM90 (Hopper: H100 and H200), SM100 (datacenter Blackwell: B200), and SM120 (consumer Blackwell: RTX 50). `require_arch` rejects any other compute-capability major. An RTX 3090 is SM86. It cannot run these kernels. If this branch is what activates, the fork is a hashrate cliff for the consumer cards in the May rental proxy, and a cost-basis reset toward Hopper and Blackwell, not only an upside tail. A secondary account of that AMA (TechFinancials, Oct 1, which dates the session 15 September) says 40-series cards were expected to drop off. We did not hear that session. The kernel list is the checkable part. The branch can still change before a release sets an activation height. That release is the trigger to re-run this call.
2. A second named duplex partner, or Together's endpoint becoming clearly live on serverless.
3. A venue with real depth (or a confirmed tier-1 listing). None in the Oct 4 ticker pull.
4. Attestable proof that a material share of hashrate does paid inference. Fees above 1% of block rewards for 30 days would be an early sign.
