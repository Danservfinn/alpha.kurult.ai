## Research view

Canton is the only chain where the user list reads like a custody desk directory: Goldman Sachs, BNY, Microsoft, Broadridge, and others are live on Daml-based privacy-enabled workflows. That adoption is verified and dated. What is not verified is what a CC token entitles the holder to inside that ecosystem. Utility payments on the network are tiny relative to the $5.1B market cap, and the token's distribution and float mechanics remain opaque to us. We watch node-operator economics and any utility-payment growth prints before taking exposure. Being early on the wrong instrument is the same as being wrong.

## First principles: the privacy primitive

Start at the primitive, not the token. Canton sells one physical property of database design: confidentiality with coordination. Ordinary shared ledgers expose every field to every participant. A bank cannot put a repo trade on a chain its competitors read. Canton's Daml model lets counterparties share only the state each is entitled to see, so two institutions can settle against a shared ledger without leaking their books. That primitive is why Goldman, BNY and Broadridge built on it. It is a real constraint solved, the way SWIFT solved message interoperability in 1973.

The economics of the primitive: institutional workflows on Canton pay for privacy and atomic settlement, and they demonstrably exist. What they do not yet pay, in any published volume, is CC tokens. The network's utility payments are tiny against a $5.1B market cap. The token is currently a claim on adoption that has not been connected to the revenue pipe. It is as if SWIFT had issued shares of a messaging protocol but the message fees were still billed in dollars elsewhere.

That is the first-principles gap and the reason for the watch rating. The chain can succeed and the token can stay idle if fee flows never route through CC. The instrument to check is not the price chart, it is a utility-payment print: node-operator payments, application fees, any recurring CC-denominated cash flow. When that number exists and grows, the token has a floor under it. Until then, a $5.1B claim on unconnected adoption is a sentiment instrument wearing an institutional suit.

## Token identity verification
The official asset is Canton Coin, ticker **CC**, not CAN. Canton Coin launched with the Global Synchronizer mainnet on July 1, 2024 and began spot trading November 10, 2025 across 11 exchanges (Kraken, Bybit, MEXC, KuCoin, Gate, others). Gate, MEXC, and KuCoin list it as "Canton (CC)" with the official uncapped mint schedule; SBI VC Trade listed it in Japan on March 25, 2026 as CC. Tokens trading as "CAN" or "Canton Network CAN" are not the official asset.

## What Canton is
Canton, built by Digital Asset (creator of the Daml smart-contract language), is a public-permissioned layer 1 for institutional finance. Its differentiator is sub-transaction privacy plus interoperability: sovereign ledgers (Goldman's GS DAP, Broadridge DLR, JPMorgan Kinexys, DTCC apps) compose atomic cross-application transactions through the decentralized Global Synchronizer. Microsoft is a founding technology partner (Azure), and the Global Synchronizer Foundation operates under Linux Foundation governance. Project Golden Gate, the DTCC/Euroclear/Goldman/BNY blueprint for onchain collateral mobility, has graduated into production programs on Canton.

## Tokenomics
Mint-and-burn model with no fixed cap: up to 100 billion CC mintable over the Global Synchronizer's first 10 years, split 50/50 between infrastructure providers (validators and super validators) and application providers, then 2.5 billion CC annually thereafter. CC pays network traffic fees, and purchased traffic is burned. September 2026 (CCView): 645.8M CC minted versus 442.6M burned (68.5% burn ratio), net issuance +203.2M CC; circulating supply ~39.7B CC, growing ~0.7% monthly. App providers take 72.8% of rewards; the top 4 validators dominate traffic purchases. A burn-focused CIP was scheduled to deploy around October 4, 2026.

## Real-world adoption (with dates)

- December 4, 2025: BNY, Nasdaq, iCapital, and S&P Global invest strategically in Digital Asset; network then reported $6T+ in onchain assets across 600+ institutions, after a June 2025 $135M round with Goldman, BNP Paribas, Citadel, DRW.
- December 17, 2025: DTCC partnership to tokenize DTC-custodied assets, backed by an SEC no-action letter.
- July 15, 2026: DTCC completes live production trades with tokenized Treasuries, equities, and ETFs on Canton with 30+ firms including BlackRock, JPMorgan, Microsoft, Nasdaq, NYSE, Vanguard, State Street.
- September-October 2026: Lloyds-Visa pilot settles ~$750K in USDC through a Canton node; TreasurySpring approved as super validator targeting ~$5B in fund shares; Canton cites $9T+ monthly tokenized RWA volume; 1,409 validators at September month-end.
- June 11, 2026: Digital Asset raises $355M led by a16z crypto with Apollo, HSBC, Coinbase Ventures, CME, SoFi.

## Bull case
DTCC's Tokenization Service launching October 2026 opens a portion of $114T in DTC-custodied assets to onchain collateral mobility; fee burns from real traffic would tighten supply under the new burn CIP; the bank and clearinghouse roster plus new crypto capital (a16z, Coinbase Ventures) is unmatched among institutional chains; SEC guidance now tolerates network buyback/burn programs.

## Bear case and risks
Value capture is unproven: mint exceeds burn monthly, supply inflates, and burned fees accrue to the protocol, not holders. Activity is flat to declining (September transactions -0.7% MoM; four-month average growth -16.5%). Much volume is piloted or subsidized rather than paid traffic. Thin liquidity plus recurring unlocks (a ~$20.7M, 0.38% unlock on September 28) add sell pressure; rival tokenization rails persist.

## 12-month outlook
Concrete catalysts: the October 2026 DTCC launch, JPMorgan Kinexys integration through 2026, and Japanese access via SBI. Fundamentals are arguably the strongest of any institutional blockchain, but price likely stays range-bound ($0.09-0.18) until fee-burn demand demonstrably outpaces the 2.5B+ annual mint. Watch quarterly traffic-purchase revenue as the key metric.
