## Research view

The asset has decoupled from its own fundamentals. ETF staking products are pulling consistent net inflows, L2 activity posts record transaction counts, and the roadmap ships on schedule. None of that has lifted the ETH/BTC ratio, which keeps making lower highs. That divergence is the trade: either the flows eventually matter, or ETH has become a yield instrument that nobody prices as growth. We lean toward the first on a 12-month window, sized so that being wrong costs the ratio, not the dollar.

## Overview
Ethereum remains the largest smart-contract platform and the #2 crypto asset by market capitalization (~$331B). After peaking at $4,946 in August 2025, ETH corrected deeply (52-week low ~$1,383) before a strong Q3 2026 recovery to ~$2,710. The 2026 narrative mixes record staking, corporate ETH treasuries, staking-enabled ETFs, and the Fusaka scaling era against a price still well below its high.

## Fundamentals and tokenomics
- **Supply:** 122.1M ETH circulating (CoinGecko, Oct 5, 2026). Issuance is low and partly burned via EIP-1559, though cheap blob space has compressed the burn.
- **Staking:** Record ~43.8M ETH staked (~36% of supply) at ~2.5% APY, with ~1.56M ETH queued - a persistent sink on liquid supply.
- **ETFs:** US spot ETH ETFs hold ~$18B AUM with ~$1.5B net inflows YTD 2026, outpacing BTC ETFs (~$985M) on relative flows, though the week of Sep 28-Oct 2 saw ~$165M in net outflows (SoSoValue/Farside data).

## Recent developments and catalysts
- **Dec 3, 2025:** Fusaka (Osaka/Fulu) activated on mainnet with PeerDAS, expanding data availability for L2s; follow-up BPO forks raised blob targets through early 2026.
- **Feb 2026:** BlackRock launched ETHB, a staking ETP (~$885M cumulative inflows, ~442K ETH, ~95% staked by early Oct).
- **Aug 2026:** Fidelity filed to stake up to 100% of its FETH ETF with quarterly cash payouts; SEC review still pending. CoinDesk (Aug 17) reported Bitmine Immersive held 4.8% of ETH supply; by late September it passed 6M ETH (~4.9%), ~85% staked.
- **Sep 28-30, 2026:** Bitmine bought $81M of ETH, its largest weekly haul since July; developers drafted a proposal for quantum-resistant validator keys.
- **Oct 6, 2026:** Glamsterdam (Amsterdam/Gloas) reaches Sepolia testnet; mainnet is targeted for late 2026 with ePBS, block-level access lists, and higher gas targets. The next upgrade (2027) has 66 candidate EIPs including FOCIL and a major privacy package.

## Market and price state
ETH trades at ~$2,710 (+0.3% 24h). Momentum is positive: +9.4% over 30 days, +42% over 60 days, +27% over 200 days. Longer windows are negative: -39.5% over 1 year, -8.8% YTD, and -45% below the August 2025 ATH of $4,946. Traders cite support near $2,500 and resistance at $2,800-$3,100.

## Bull case
- Supply lock-up: ~36% staked plus treasuries (Bitmine alone ~4.9% of supply) shrink the sellable float.
- Institutional rails: staking ETFs (ETHB live, FETH pending) and ETH ETF inflows outpacing BTC ETFs YTD.
- RWA dominance: ~93% of tokenized US assets settle on Ethereum; ETH carries roughly a third of the ~$65B RWA market. L2s (Base ~$16.5B, Arbitrum ~$11.9B TVS) handle 95%+ of ecosystem activity; Robinhood Chain passed $1B TVL.
- Catalysts: Glamsterdam mainnet; Street targets include Tom Lee's $6K "conservative" year-end call and Arthur Hayes' $10K.

## Bear case and risks
- Still a structural drawdown: -45% from ATH with negative 1Y and YTD returns and weak ETH/BTC.
- Treasury reflexivity: Bitmine's cost basis (~$3,300) sits above spot; stress could force selling into a thin market.
- Value capture: near-zero blob fees weaken the burn and the "ultrasound money" thesis; Glamsterdam must revive L1 economics.
- Competition (Solana, appchains), macro risk, regulatory reversal on ETF staking, and upgrade execution risk.

## Outlook (12 months)
Constructive but volatile. Glamsterdam activation, a possible FETH staking approval, continued ETF/treasury absorption, and RWA growth support a push toward $3,500-$4,500 by late 2027, with $2,500 as key support. A breakout above $3,100 would signal a retest of the ATH zone; failure risks $2,000-$2,200. Watch ETF flow persistence, post-Glamsterdam blob fee burn, and treasury-company health.
