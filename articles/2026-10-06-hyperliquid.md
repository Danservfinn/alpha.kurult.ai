## Overview
Hyperliquid is a purpose-built Layer 1 whose flagship product is a fully on-chain order-book perpetuals exchange (HyperCore), paired with HyperEVM, an EVM layer that reads the exchange's live state. As of October 5, 2026, HYPE ranks 11th by market cap (about $21.0B) and Hyperliquid is the dominant on-chain derivatives venue, with about $197B in 30-day perp volume per DeFiLlama, several times the next chains (Arbitrum, Solana, Lighter each near $45-47B). TVL stands at $7.3B.

## Fundamentals and tokenomics
- Max supply 1B HYPE; about 222.4M circulating (22%), FDV about $90B (CoinGecko, Oct 5).
- The Assistance Fund converts trading fees into HYPE and burns it. Cumulative burns: about 49.25M HYPE (about $4.45B), including 112,580 HYPE (about $10.15M) burned on October 5.
- Confirmed (DeFiLlama, Oct 5): $68.5M fees and $53.0M revenue in 30 days; about $681M annualized earnings; $5.44T cumulative perp volume; open interest $8.4B.
- AQAv2 (approved June 12, 2026, 69.08% of the vote) routes about 90% of USDC reserve yield to the protocol. First payment of $14.58M arrived October 3, covering August 26 to September 24. Estimates of roughly $193M per year, lifting total buyback capacity above $900M, are extrapolations, not confirmed run-rates. Coinbase is treasury deployer, Circle technical deployer; both staked 500K HYPE.

## Recent developments and catalysts
- Sept 10: BitGo self-custody wallets can connect to Hyperliquid via WalletConnect.
- Sept 16: Kraken parent Payward announced plans for permissioned US-client perps on Hyperliquid under Bitnomial rules, pending approval, no launch date.
- Sept 18: manual USDC/USDT borrowing went live on HyperCore (HYPE 65% LTV, BTC 50%); about $269M in loans within days.
- Sept 23: HYPE hit its all-time high of $97.96; open interest peaked near $14-18B across sources.
- Oct 3-5: first AQAv2 payment and a record single-day burn.
- Oct 5: Bloomberg Terminal began carrying selected Hyperliquid perp prices (WSL HYPE); President Trump said the CFTC is working to bring Hyperliquid to the US. Data availability is confirmed; any US regulatory outcome is not.
- HyperEVM holds about $5.8B in stablecoins, up 96% since February; HIP-3 builder markets are about 30% of volume.

## Market and price state
HYPE trades at $94.53 (CoinGecko, Oct 5), down 3.5% from the $97.96 ATH set September 23. It is up about 272% YTD (from $25.44 on January 1), up about 92-97% over one year, and up from $36.49 on April 1 and $64.97 on July 1. Traders cite support at $89-90 and resistance at $94-97.

## Bull case
Real fee-funded deflation, now partly volume-independent via reserve yield; institutional traction (Bloomberg data, Kraken/Bitnomial plan, BitGo custody routing); about 58% DEX-perp market share; Hyperliquid Strategies holds 33.6M HYPE (about 15% of float); Polymarket prices 71.5% odds of $100 by December 31 (speculative).

## Bear case and risks
FDV of $90B versus $21B market cap implies a large unlock overhang; fee revenue is cyclical and the $900M buyback figure is an annualized estimate; HyperEVM throughput limits pushed Kinetiq to build elsewhere; rising competition (Lighter, edgeX); selling by Multicoin Capital ($12.15M to Coinbase Prime) and Wintermute; valuation near 31x annualized earnings leaves little cushion; US regulatory acceptance unconfirmed.

## Outlook (12 months)
Speculative. Confirmed catalysts (AQAv2 settlement cadence, Kraken's US launch if approved, continued burns) support the structural bull case, while unlocks, fee cyclicality, and a full valuation argue for volatility. A $60-120 range over 12 months is plausible, hinging on whether reserve-yield buybacks prove durable.
