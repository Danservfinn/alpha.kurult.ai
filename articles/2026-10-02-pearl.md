## The call in one paragraph

Pearl is the most serious proof-of-useful-work design we have reviewed, and the team is unusually credible. The token is a different question. It has re-rated about 4x to 6x on narrative since late August (CoinGecko daily prices at 00:00 UTC: 4.3x from Aug 20 to Oct 2, 5.7x from Aug 20 to the Sep 28 high). On-chain usage has roughly doubled, and fees are close to zero. Usefulness is enabled by the protocol but not enforced. Supply grows about 27% in three months and about 92% in twelve. Reported spot liquidity sits on two low-trust venues. Our research view at about $1.11 is **avoid an outright long (medium conviction)**. The bull case depends almost entirely on the FP8 / Pearl-GEMM upgrade shipping and on verifiable demand for useful mining.

## What Pearl is

Pearl is a proof-of-work Layer 1 derived from Bitcoin. Mainnet launched 2026-04-27. It replaces SHA-256 with noised matrix multiplication, so GPU work that serves AI inference can mine PRL at the same time. Pearl calls this "duplex compute". The core construction is in arXiv 2504.09971. Max supply is 2.1B PRL, with a smooth emission curve that puts half the supply out by block 650,226. Hashrate Index describes "roughly two-minute blocks" and a "2.1 billion PRL max supply". The explorer reports a 194 second target.

Sources: arXiv 2504.09971; pearlchain.live stats (block reward, 2026-10-02 19:33 ET); @WeinsteinOmri on X (overhead); arXiv 2606.04819. Simplified schematic.")

- **Team:** Omri Weinstein (ex-Nvidia, Vast Data), Ilan Komargodski, Idan Sugerman. Rafael Pass is research scientist and advisor (economics paper arXiv 2606.06700). The team claims no premine and no VC token allocation.
- **Code:** the GitHub repo was created 2026-04-19 and was active on Oct 2 (335 stars, 79 forks, 95 open issues, read about 11:46 ET). Recent releases: v1.4.1 (Aug 11, emergency "Salted noise-seed" hardfork), v1.4.8 (Sep 16), v1.4.11 (Sep 29). Three committers since Sep 1. The FP8 pull request (#311) was still open.
- **Partner:** Together AI's May 15 blog announced an "exclusive partnership". It said the Gemma-4-31B-it-Pearl endpoint "is discounted by over 25%, offset by the future value of crypto emissions" (page read 16:07 ET). The model page lists $0.28 input and $0.86 output per 1M tokens (INT8, 32K context). The same page states "This model is not available on Together's Serverless API" (read 16:07 ET). So the discount is real on paper, but live availability is unclear.
- **Pearl's own inference product** at pearlresearch.ai is priced in USD, not PRL. Today there is no token sink.

## Miner economics

- **Revenue:** at $1.11, issuance is about $1.15M to $1.18M per day (unconfirmed issuance, Sep 29 to Oct 1).
- **Cost anchors:** prlstats.com (@jeremiahrogers, Sep 26, at about 59 EH/s) estimated $2.24 per PRL for synthetic mining on frontier hardware, $0.68 on idle owned GPUs and $1.31 during inference. arXiv 2606.04819 (June, 24 EH/s, PRL at $0.76) put breakeven at $0.46 to $0.77 by GPU tier.
- **Scaled to today:** scaling the prlstats figures to the unconfirmed 42.2 EH/s gives roughly $1.60, $0.49 and $0.94 (derived).
- **Read:** the marginal rented miner is near breakeven at today's price. Owned hardware has much lower cash costs, so most issuance is likely sold whatever the price. Together's own framing, an endpoint discount "offset by the future value of crypto emissions", implies the same thing for duplex miners.
- **Overhead:** @WeinsteinOmri says the current protocol is "INT-8 only, and adds 10-15% overhead". The new Pearl-GEMM targets 0 to 3% and is "expected to launch within weeks". He also says it "will always allow spam mining". arXiv 2606.04819 found no inference strings in the dominant pool's mining software (May 30 to 31).

## Catalysts and risks

Catalysts:

1. FP8 / Pearl-GEMM hardfork. Described as "within weeks", with no date. This is the main fundamental catalyst.
2. Hyperliquid HIP-3 perp launch. Two-sided: it brings attention, but also a liquid venue for miner hedging and for short sellers.
3. A tier-1 exchange listing. None confirmed.
4. More duplex partners, or Together's endpoint becoming clearly live.
5. Attestable proof of what share of hashrate does useful work.

Risks:

- Dilution: +27% supply in 3 months and +92% in 12 (unconfirmed supply).
- Thin, low-trust venues.
- Pool concentration: one payee found 52.5% of the last 200 blocks.
- Launch-week supply concentration.
- Rentable, merge-mined hashrate, and two consensus exploits so far.
- An upgradeable bridge.
- Promotion-driven flows.
- Ticker collisions.

## What would change the view

Bullish falsifiers:

- FP8 live on mainnet with measured end-to-end overhead of 3% or less, and within about 60 days attestable data showing over 30% of hashrate doing paid inference.
- A second named production duplex partner.
- PRL holding above $1.80 for five daily closes after a Hyperliquid listing.
- Fees above 1% of block rewards over 30 days.

Bearish confirmations:

- FP8 not on mainnet by Dec 31, 2026.
- A single payee above 50% over 1,000 blocks.
- Non-coinbase transactions under 10,000 per day while price holds.
- Large dormant wallets moving to venues.

## Scenarios (research only, subjective probabilities)

These scenarios use supply of about 422M at 3 months and about 640M at 12 months (unconfirmed).

- **3 months, bear (40%):** $0.35 to $0.70. FP8 slips, miners keep selling into thin volume, and price reverts toward miner cost anchors.
- **3 months, base (40%):** $0.80 to $1.40. FP8 is announced but not decisive. Market cap grows slower than supply.
- **3 months, bull (20%):** $1.80 to $3.00. FP8 ships on time with a tier-1 listing or a liquid perp, plus a second duplex partner.
- **12 months, bear (45%):** $0.15 to $0.45. Usefulness stays unproven, and supply roughly doubles into fading attention.
